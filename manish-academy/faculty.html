  • Mentor to 200+ IIT rank holders since 2009

Head of Department · Biology (NEET)

MBBS from AIIMS Delhi. Has taught NEET Biology since 2011. Specialises in Genetics, Ecology and Human Physiology. Her chapter notes have become the unofficial Bible for NEET prep among alumni.

  • MBBS, AIIMS New Delhi

  • Chief mentor for NEET Dropper Batch since 2015
  • 100+ students in AIIMS & JIPMER since 2011

Chemistry HOD (JEE + NEET)
IIT Bombay 16 yrs Organic & Physical

  • 16+ years teaching JEE & NEET Chemistry
  • Author, Organic Chemistry Simplified (2020)
  • Columnist, Careers360 Chemistry section

Mathematics (JEE & Foundation)

Gold medalist in Mathematics from IIT Madras. Heads the Foundation programme for Classes VIII–X and teaches Calculus for JEE. Also coaches our Olympiad and NTSE students every weekend.

  • M.Sc. Mathematics, IIT Madras (Gold Medal)
  • 12+ years teaching experience
  • Olympiad & NTSE programme head since 2016
  • Mentored 14 NTSE scholars since 2017

Physics (NEET) & Board Preparation
