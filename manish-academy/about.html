Our Story

From a single
classroom to a legacy.

When Manish Kumar — an IIT Delhi alumnus with a decade of teaching behind him — started this academy in 2009, he had one rule: never teach more students than you can remember by name.

That rule stays. Today, across three floors in the heart of Delhi, we welcome a new batch each year and treat them the way we did our very first — with care, with rigour, and with the belief that every one of them deserves our best.

The results show. Over 5,200 students mentored. Over 500 selections into IITs, NITs, and AIIMS. And a waiting list we're proud of — because it means we haven't compromised to grow.

Manish Kumar
Founder & Director · IIT Delhi '03
What We Stand For

Our compass,
unchanged.

Three principles we've never bent on, no matter how the coaching industry has changed around us.

Our Mission

Teach the student, not the syllabus.

Every learner walks in with a different story, a different pace, a different weak spot. Our job isn't to cover chapters — it's to meet each student where they are and take them further than they believed possible.

  • Personal mentor assigned to every student
  • Weekly progress tracking and 1-on-1 reviews
  • Customised study plans for droppers and repeaters
Our Vision

Make serious coaching accessible.

Top coaching shouldn't be a privilege of the few. We run a merit-based scholarship program that waives up to 100% of tuition for students who qualify — because ambition doesn't check your income bracket.

  • Annual scholarship test for Class 9, 10 & 11 aspirants
  • Up to 100% fee waiver for top performers
  • Flexible instalment plans for every family
Our Values

Integrity, then everything else.

We don't inflate our numbers. We don't recycle faculty photos. We don't promise ranks. What we do promise is honest teaching, transparent fees, and a team that picks up the phone when you call.

  • Published results — student names verifiable
  • No hidden fees, ever
  • Full refund policy within the first 15 days
