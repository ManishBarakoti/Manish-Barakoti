Our Story

From a rented classroom to a trusted family institution.

When Manish Kumar — an IIT Delhi alumnus with a decade of teaching behind him — started this academy in June 2009, he had exactly 15 students, one borrowed projector, and a list of principles written on the back of an admission form.

That list still hangs, framed, in the reception. It says: never more than 25 students per class, never hire a teacher who hasn't cracked the exam themselves, never promise a rank we can't back with data, and never turn away a deserving student over money.

Sixteen years later, the institute has expanded into three floors of the same building. The admission test is still conducted personally by the founder. And 72% of new admissions still come through referrals from parents and alumni — the only metric we truly track.

What We Stand For

Three principles. Zero compromises.

The three things we've never bent on, regardless of how the coaching industry has changed around us.

Our Mission

Teach the student, not the syllabus.

Every learner arrives with a different story, a different pace, a different weak spot. Our job is to meet each student where they are and take them further than they thought possible.

  • Personal mentor assigned from day one
  • Weekly progress tracking and 1-on-1 reviews every Saturday
  • Customised study plans for droppers and repeaters
  • Direct faculty WhatsApp access, Mon–Sat
Our Vision

Serious coaching shouldn't be a privilege.

Top coaching has become too expensive for most middle-class Indian families. Our annual scholarship test waives up to 100% of tuition for deserving students — 248 received scholarships in 2025 alone.

  • Annual scholarship test (first Sunday of June)
  • Up to 100% fee waiver for top performers
  • Flexible instalments — 2, 3 or 4 payments, zero interest
  • ₹1.2 Cr+ in scholarships awarded in 2025
Our Values

Honesty first, then everything else.

We don't inflate our numbers. We don't recycle faculty photos. We don't promise ranks. We publish student names on our results page and give every family a 15-day refund window — no questions asked.

  • Published results — every name verifiable with the exam board
  • No hidden fees, ever — written fee structure at admission
  • Full refund policy within 15 days of first class
  • Transparent batch capacity published weekly
