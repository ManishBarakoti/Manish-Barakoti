Our Story

From a Small Classroom to a Trusted Institution

Manish Academy was founded in 2009 by a team of passionate educators with one simple belief: every student deserves access to quality coaching that is both affordable and effective. What started as a small classroom of 15 students has grown into one of the most respected coaching institutes, having trained over 5,000 students across JEE, NEET, Foundation, and Board Exams.

Our approach combines rigorous academic training with mentorship, emotional support, and strategic exam preparation. We don't just teach subjects, we teach students how to think critically, solve problems independently, and stay motivated through their preparation journey.

Purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by a clear purpose, we work every day to make quality education accessible to every ambitious learner.

Our Mission

To empower every student with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to excel in competitive examinations and build a successful career. We're committed to personalized, affordable, and results-driven coaching for all.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted coaching institute — known for academic excellence, student-centric teaching, and lifelong learning outcomes. We envision a future where every deserving student has the opportunity to achieve their full potential.

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, empathy, and innovation drive everything we do. We believe in transparent communication, continuous improvement, and creating a supportive environment where every learner can thrive and grow.
