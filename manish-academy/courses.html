How It Works

Four steps
to get started.

No complicated admissions process. Just a simple, transparent path from your first demo to your first class.

Step 01

Book a free counselling session.

Meet one of our academic advisors — in-person or over a call. Tell us your goals, your current class, your strengths and struggles. We'll help you understand exactly where you stand.

  • Free 30-minute consultation
  • Honest assessment, no sales pitch
  • Written plan sent to you after the call
Step 02

Sit in on a live demo class.

Every program comes with a free demo class. Sit in the actual classroom, experience the faculty's teaching style, and meet the students you'd learn alongside.

  • 90-minute live demo with actual faculty
  • Sample study material handed out
  • Q&A with current batch students
Step 03

Enrol on your own terms.

Pay in full for an extra discount, or split your fees across 2, 3, or 4 instalments. No interest, no hidden charges, and a 15-day full refund window if it's not the right fit.

  • Up to 100% scholarship via entrance test
  • Flexible instalment plans, zero interest
  • 15-day no-questions-asked refund policy
