Why Manish Academy

A different kind
of coaching.

We don't run a factory. We run a classroom — where every student is known by name, every doubt is worth answering, and every result is earned.

01 — Teaching

Faculty who've sat in your seat.

Every subject is led by an IIT, NIT, or AIIMS alumnus — educators who cracked these exams themselves and have spent a decade teaching students how to do the same. No freshers. No shortcuts.

  • Average faculty experience: 12+ years
  • All core faculty are IIT/NIT/AIIMS graduates
  • Unlimited 1-on-1 doubt sessions, in-person or online
02 — Batches

25 students. Not 250.

We cap every classroom at 25 learners. That means you get eye contact with the teacher, space to ask questions, and a mentor who actually knows whether you understood yesterday's chapter.

  • Maximum 25 students per batch — guaranteed
  • Personal mentor assigned to every student
  • Weekly one-on-one progress reviews
03 — Material

Material you'll actually use.

Our in-house study material is crafted chapter-by-chapter by subject heads — printed books, chapter-wise worksheets, previous year archives, and video explanations. Built for the real exam, not for a shelf.

  • 20,000+ curated practice problems with solutions
  • Chapter tests + full-length mocks on exam pattern
  • Detailed performance analytics every week
