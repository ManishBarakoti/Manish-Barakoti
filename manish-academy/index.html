About Us

A 16-year legacy of teaching, not a coaching chain.

Manish Academy started in 2009 with 15 students in a single rented classroom at Karol Bagh. Today, we operate from the same building — expanded across three floors, but still run by the same core team, with the same rule: never more than 25 students in a batch.

We don't franchise. We don't advertise on WhatsApp. We don't run on celebrity faculty. What we do run is a transparent, rigorous classroom — which is why 7 out of 10 of our students come from a parent or sibling referral.

Why Parents Choose Us

Built around the student, not the syllabus.

Every part of our programme — from batch size to mentor meetings — is designed so no student gets lost in the crowd. Here's what you get on day one.

01 · Teaching

Full-time faculty from IIT, AIIMS, NIT

Every subject is taught by a senior educator who sat through the same exam and has spent 10–18 years teaching it since. No freshers. No part-timers. No “recorded lectures” played in class.

  • Average faculty experience: 12+ years
  • Every core teacher is an IIT / AIIMS / NIT alumnus
  • Unlimited 1-on-1 doubt sessions (in-person or online)
  • Faculty names and credentials published on our site
02 · Batch Size

25 students per batch. Guaranteed.

We cap every classroom at 25 learners, in writing. If admissions cross that number, we open a new batch with a second teacher. This is the single hardest rule we've held onto since 2009.

  • Written 25-student cap in your admission letter
  • Dedicated personal mentor for every student
  • Weekly 1-on-1 progress review (every Saturday)
  • Direct WhatsApp access to faculty, Mon–Sat
03 · Study Material

In-house material, tested across 15 batches

Our printed study modules are written by our own subject heads — not licensed from a publisher. 22,000+ problems, chapter-wise tests, previous year archives from 2005, and video solutions for every PYQ.

  • 22,000+ curated practice problems with worked solutions
  • Weekly chapter tests + 36 full-length mocks per year
  • PYQ archive for JEE (2005–2025) and NEET (2013–2025)
  • Detailed performance report mailed every Monday
