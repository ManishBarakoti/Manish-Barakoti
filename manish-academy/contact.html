FAQs

Questions, answered honestly.

The most common questions parents and students ask us — with straight answers, not marketing copy.

Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?

Yes, always. Every programme comes with a free demo — a full 90-minute live class with the actual faculty who will teach your child. Walk in on any Saturday at 11 AM or 3 PM, or book a weekday demo through the form above.

Do you offer scholarships? How much can I save?

We run an annual Scholarship Test on the first Sunday of June, open to students entering Classes IX, X, and XI. Top 10% of scorers receive 50–100% tuition waivers. In 2025, 248 students received scholarships worth ₹1.2 Cr in total.

What if I want a refund after enrolling?

We offer a 15-day no-questions-asked refund window from the date of your first class. Full tuition refund, minus a small processing fee of ₹2,000. We hand this policy in writing at the time of admission.

Can I pay fees in instalments?

Yes. We offer 2, 3, or 4-instalment plans across the academic year with zero interest. No credit check. First instalment at admission, rest quarterly. We also accept cheques, UPI, and cards.

Is there an online-only option?

Our flagship JEE, NEET, and Crash courses are available in live-online mode alongside the offline batches. Study material, weekly tests, and doubt sessions are identical. Live sessions are also recorded for revision.

How are batches sized? What if my child joins late?

We cap every batch at 25 students in writing. If a class exceeds that, we open a new batch with a second faculty member. Late joiners (within the first month) receive one-on-one catch-up sessions at no extra charge.

Who owns the institute? Is it a franchise?

Manish Academy is fully owned and operated by its founder, Mr. Manish Kumar (IIT Delhi '03). It is registered under MSME, Government of India, and is ISO 9001:2015 certified. We do not franchise and have only one campus — Karol Bagh, New Delhi.
