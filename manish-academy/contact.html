FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from students and parents.

Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?

Yes. We offer a free demo class for every course. Simply fill out the contact form or call us to schedule one at your convenience.

Do you offer scholarships?

We offer merit-based scholarships up to 100% tuition through our entrance test. Details are shared during counselling.

Is there an online option?

Yes. Most of our flagship courses (JEE, NEET, Crash) are available in both offline and live online modes.

Can I pay fees in instalments?

Yes, we offer flexible instalment plans of 2, 3, or 4 parts across the academic year — no interest charged.
